# Open-Droid-Lab
Hello and Welcome to the Open Droid Lab.
This is your go-to for all things droid related.
My name is Eric Young and I have been working with arduino, esp32, sensors, actuators and displays for the past few years building proofs of concept for my own personal amusement and education.
During the past year I have been working with ROS2 to create a brain/network path with the goal to create a droid that uses sensor input to interact with its environment in a meaningful way/manner.
As a former, full-time martial arts instructor I have always striven to make seemingly  difficult concepts more manageable and easier for others to grasp.
This is a starting point of a journey that has a goal of getting a droid up and running.
What does this mean?
Sensors will take in data, pass it to a raspberry pi brain using ROS2 that determines behaviors and output those behaviors via displays and actuators… in short, a droid that ‘responds to its environment’.
This could result in a robot companion/assistant.
Open Droid Lab is intended to be a lab + build + a public record of the work as it moves forward.
OUR GOAL: to provide real, reproducible steps that move from single sensor data into working robot behaviors
This aims to go well beyond novelty and into transferrable skills, confidence that comes from building real systems, and helping instill confidence in the developing skill set of those who decide to follow along.
The weekly schedule is intended to show regular public progress, lessons, code, photos and short videos.
You can get involved or follow along by watching, reading, cloning the repo, comment and mimic the steps that I take.
I would encourage you to participate in the process rather than treat this as disposable content. Writing your own thoughts as you follow along will only benefit you in your growth.

The pace is intended to be a stepwise process where we begin with a plan for the week, physical build & code progress with the intention of having a working product by weeks end.
I will NOT provide 1-on-1 support, a finished commercial product at this level.
Respectful comments of those who are honestly trying to learn the content and understand the deeper lessons will be given priority with rude/demeaning comments will not receive any kind of priority.
We aim to develop SOLUTIONS… NOT create more problems, consternation or drama

They say that a rising tide raises all ships. Let’s raise that tide together.
